Day 1 · 28 March 2026

Colombo → Bentota

Arrival · Mangrove River · First Beach Sunset 94 km
  • ✈️
    11:05 AM — Land at Colombo Airport (CMB)

    Get a SIM card right inside arrivals — Mobitel or Dialog both work great. Download the PickMe app — it's like Uber for tuk tuks!

  • 🥥
    King Coconut outside arrivals

    Bright orange coconuts called "thambili" — they taste incredible and cost about INR 28. Your first taste of Sri Lanka!

    FREE
  • ⛽
    PETROL STOP — Fill up FULL near airport

    CEYPETCO or IOC station on Galle Road before leaving Colombo. Fill the whole tank — it's only a 7.5 litre tank!

    Petrol Stop 1 · INR 645
  • 🛺
    2:30 PM — Drive south to Bentota

    94 km down the beautiful Galle Road coastal highway. About 2.5 hours in the tuk tuk. Keep the ocean on your right!

    📏 94 km · ~2.5 hrs
  • 🏖️
    5:00 PM — Bentota River Mouth Beach Walk

    A 45-minute walk where the river meets the ocean. The sun turns everything golden at this hour. This is your first proper Sri Lanka beach moment!

    FREE
  • 🚣
    6:00 PM — Mangrove River Boat Ride

    A 1-hour boat safari through the mangrove jungle. You'll spot monitor lizards sunbathing on roots, kingfishers darting like jewels, and giant fruit bats taking off at dusk. Ask your hotel to arrange it.

    ~INR 1,700/boat
  • 🍛
    7:30 PM — Dinner: Local Rice & Curry

    Order rice and curry at any local spot. Say "without fish, without meat" clearly. Sri Lankan rice & curry is just warm, spicy heaven.

📍 Google Maps
Colombo Airport (CMB) Bentota River Beach Mangrove Boat Safari
💡 Tips
💡Lunch is served on the SriLankan Airlines flight — no need to stop for lunch today!
💡The mangrove boat is best arranged through your Bentota hotel — ask at check-in.
💡Carry small notes for tips. Tuk tuk drivers and boat operators love it!
Day 2 · 29 March 2026

Bentota → Galle → Weligama

Bawa Gardens · Baby Turtles · UNESCO Fort · Coastal Drive ~98 km
  • 🏊
    7:00 AM — Morning swim at Bentota Beach

    30 min in the cool morning ocean. Barely anyone around — pure bliss before the day begins.

  • 🌿
    7:45 AM — Lunuganga Estate (Geoffrey Bawa)

    Sri Lanka's most famous architect built this dreamy garden on a rubber estate — terraced lawns, ancient trees, colonial house. Like walking into a painting.

    Entry: $3 (INR 255)📏 From hotel · 15 min
  • 🎨
    10:00 AM — Brief Garden (Bevis Bawa)

    Geoffrey's eccentric brother Bevis created this surrealist jungle full of strange sculptures and whimsical art. 1.5 hrs to wander around properly.

    Entry: $3 (INR 255)📏 12 km · 20 min
  • 🐢
    11:45 AM — Kosgoda Turtle Conservation

    Baby sea turtles the size of your palm! They hatch eggs here and release the babies into the ocean. You can hold one gently.

    Entry: $5 (INR 425)📏 10 km · 15 min
  • 🏄
    1:00 PM — Hikkaduwa Beach & Lunch

    Great snorkelling reef right off the beach. Check the menu for vegetarian options — it's a seafood-heavy area. Buy lunch here before heading south.

    Beach FREE⛽ Petrol Stop 2 here — INR 335
  • 🏰
    3:30 PM — Unawatuna Beach

    A perfect horseshoe bay — calm water, beautiful curve of sand. Safe for swimming. 1 hour stop.

    FREE📏 25 km · 30 min
  • ⚓
    4:45 PM — Galle Dutch Fort at Sunset

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site — an entire colonial town inside ancient walls. Walk the ramparts as the sun drops over the ocean. The lighthouse glows gold. The cafes inside are great for vegetarians!

    FREE entry📏 6 km · 10 min
  • 🚗
    7:15 PM — Drive to Weligama

    40 km, about 1 hour. Check in, rest up — tomorrow you're hunting blue whales.

    📏 40 km · 1 hr
📍 Google Maps
Lunuganga Estate Brief Garden (Bevis Bawa) Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery Hikkaduwa Beach Unawatuna Beach Galle Dutch Fort
💡 Tips
💡Busiest driving day — start early at 7 AM sharp!
💡At Galle Fort, walk the ocean-facing rampart wall — it's the best spot at sunset.
💡Vegetarian cafes inside Galle Fort: The Heritage and Pedlar's Inn are both excellent.
Day 3 · 30 March 2026

Weligama — The Big Day

🐋 Whale Watching · 🏄 Surfing · Parrot Rock Sunset 0 km driving
  • 🐋
    6:00 AM — WHALE WATCHING (3–5 hours at sea!)

    Blue whales are the biggest animals that have EVER lived on Earth — longer than 3 city buses parked end to end. March/April is the absolute best time to see them off Mirissa. The boat goes far out into deep ocean water. Bring sunscreen!

    ~$50/person (INR 4,250)📞 Book ahead!
  • 🥤
    Important: Confirm vegetarian snacks when booking

    The boat serves breakfast — confirm no fish or meat products. Raja & Whales: +94 71 046 0399. Book at least 2 weeks before travel!

  • 🍽️
    11:30 AM — Return, shower, rest & lunch

    Vegetarian kottu roti — chopped flatbread stir-fried with vegetables, eggs, and spices on a hot iron plate. The sound of the iron scrapers is part of the experience!

  • 🏄
    1:30 PM — Surf Lesson at Weligama Beach

    Weligama is called the best beginner surf beach in all of Asia. The waves are gentle, the instructors are brilliant, and you WILL stand up on your first day. 1.5 hour lesson.

    ~INR 1,700/person
  • 🌴
    3:30 PM — Explore Weligama Town

    Wander the streets, try fresh tropical fruits, buy a coconut, watch stilt fishermen if you can spot them from the shore.

  • 🦜
    5:15 PM — Parrot Rock Golden Hour

    A giant rock island just off the beach that turns deep orange in the sunset. Sit on the beach and watch the colours change. One of Sri Lanka's most iconic sunset views.

    FREE
  • 🍛
    7:00 PM — Dinner

    Ask for pumpkin curry, dhal, pol sambol, and string hoppers. Best vegetarian combo in Sri Lanka.

📍 Google Maps
Mirissa Harbour (Whale Watching) Weligama Beach (Surf) Parrot Rock, Weligama
💡 Tips
🐋Book whale watching at least 2 weeks in advance. March/April = peak blue whale season. You are very lucky!
💡No driving today — rest your tuk tuk arms! Weligama is small enough to walk everywhere.
💡Bring motion sickness tablets if either of you gets seasick — the whale boat goes to deep water.
Day 4 · 31 March 2026

Into the Mountains

Coconut Hill · Hiriketiya · Ravana Falls ⭐ · Diyaluma ~168 km
  • 🌅
    6:00 AM — Coconut Tree Hill, Mirissa

    A small hill completely covered in tall coconut trees that lean dramatically out over the cliff. Watch the sun rise out of the ocean from here. Absolutely breathtaking — one of those views you'll never forget.

    FREE📏 7 km · 15 min
  • ⛵
    6:45 AM — Mirissa Fishing Harbour

    Walk down from Coconut Hill to watch fishermen bringing in the morning catch as the golden sun paints the colourful boats. A quiet magical moment.

    FREE📏 10 min walk from Coconut Hill
  • 🍳
    7:30 AM — Breakfast + Check Out

    Egg hoppers (bowl-shaped crispy crepes with egg cooked inside) + pol sambol (spiced coconut paste). The best Sri Lankan breakfast.

  • 🏊
    10:15 AM — Hiriketiya Beach

    A SECRET hidden cove only discovered recently — the ocean wraps around like a perfect turquoise bowl. Shallow, calm, beautiful. One of the most unique beaches in all of Asia.

    FREE📏 45 km · 1 hr
  • 🌊
    12:00 PM — Tangalle Beach & Lunch

    Beautiful wide beach. IMPORTANT for the vegetarian: say "without fish, without meat, without Maldive fish" very clearly — Tangalle is an intense seafood town and hidden fish seasoning is everywhere.

    ⛽ Petrol Stop 3 here — INR 430📏 25 km · 35 min
  • 🗿
    3:00 PM — Buduruwagala Rock Carvings

    Seven giant Buddhas carved directly into a jungle rock face 1,200 years ago. They're enormous — the central standing Buddha is 15 metres tall! Hidden behind a quiet forest path.

    Entry: $5 (INR 425)📏 60 km · 1.5 hrs
  • 💧
    NEW ⭐ — Ravana Falls (20 min stop)

    A stunning 25-metre waterfall that you can see RIGHT FROM THE ROAD — just pull the tuk tuk over! Named after the demon king Ravana from the Ramayana. Totally free, zero effort, completely beautiful.

    FREE📏 20 km · 30 min⭐ NEW
  • 🏨
    4:30 PM — Diyaluma Falls & Check In

    Arrive at your guesthouse near Sri Lanka's second-tallest waterfall. Eat early, sleep early — tomorrow morning you'll be swimming at the TOP of it.

    📏 15 km · 20 min
📍 Google Maps
Coconut Tree Hill, Mirissa Mirissa Fishing Harbour Hiriketiya Beach Tangalle Beach Buduruwagala Rock Carvings Ravana Falls ⭐ Diyaluma Falls
💡 Tips
💡Longest driving day — 168 km. Start at 6 AM sharp to make it comfortably.
💡Ravana Falls needs zero effort — just pull over on the A23 road and stare!
🥗Tangalle is a seafood town — be extra specific about vegetarian needs here.
Day 5 · 1 April 2026

Highland Wonders

Infinity Pools · Bambarakanda · Lipton's Seat ⭐ · First Abseil! ~110 km
  • 🌊
    6:30 AM — Upper Diyaluma Infinity Pools

    Natural swimming pools right at the edge of a 220-metre cliff! You literally swim and look DOWN at the whole valley. Take the Poonagala road to the top, then 30 minutes walk down to the pools. Guide Saman knows every pool!

    Guide fee ~INR 255📞 Saman: +94 71 430 4910
  • 🍳
    8:15 AM — Breakfast + Check Out

    Ask for early breakfast at 8 AM when you check in. String hoppers are perfect highland fuel.

  • 🏔️
    11:00 AM — Bambarakanda Falls

    Sri Lanka's TALLEST waterfall at 263 metres — taller than the Eiffel Tower! It's a thin silver ribbon dropping from the sky into a misty pool. Quick 45-minute stop — photos and awe, then move on.

    Entry: LKR 350 (INR 97)📏 40 km · 1 hr
  • 🍵
    NEW ⭐ — 12:30 PM — Lipton's Seat Tea Estate

    Drive through endless rolling green tea plantations to a hilltop where Sir Thomas Lipton (the tea brand!) used to watch over his entire estate. On a clear April day you can see all the way to the southern coast. The road up through the Dambatenne Tea Factory is one of the most scenic drives in Sri Lanka.

    FREE📏 30 km · 45 min⭐ NEW
  • 🍛
    1:30 PM — Maskeliya Lunch

    Vegetarian rice and curry in the highlands is exceptional — the pumpkin, dhal, and jackfruit curries up here are spiced differently than the coast. Best vegetarian food of the trip!

  • 🧗
    2:30 PM — Gartmore Falls Abseil 25m!

    Your FIRST abseil! Walk backwards off a 25-metre waterfall into a pool overlooking the Maskeliya Reservoir. It sounds terrifying but the guides are incredible. This is the confidence-builder before the big one tomorrow!

    ~$30/person (INR 2,550)📞 Ceylon Extreme: +94 70 7900 700
  • 🚶
    4:15 PM — Maskeliya Reservoir Walk

    A serene lake completely surrounded by misty tea-estate mountains. 45 minutes strolling the edge with the cool highland air around you.

    FREE
  • 🏨
    5:15 PM — Check In Maskeliya/Hatton

    Dinner: string hoppers + pol sambol + dhal + pumpkin curry. The absolute BEST vegetarian meal of the entire trip. Sleep well — tomorrow is the BIG abseil!

    ⛽ Petrol Stop 4 & 5 · Wellawaya + Hatton · INR 415+335
📍 Google Maps
Upper Diyaluma Pools Bambarakanda Falls Lipton's Seat ⭐ Gartmore Falls Maskeliya Reservoir
💡 Tips
💡Book Gartmore AND Laxapana together: Ceylon Extreme +94 70 7900 700.
🍵Lipton's Seat is best in the morning — afternoon clouds can cover the view.
💡It gets cold up here at night — pack a light jacket in the tuk tuk from Day 4 onwards!
Day 6 · 2 April 2026

The Big Abseil Day

🧗 Laxapana 126m · Prehistoric Cave · Rainforest · Rock Pools ~80 km
  • 🧗
    8:00 AM — LAXAPANA FALLS ABSEIL · 126 METRES!!

    THE moment of the trip. The highest free-falling waterfall abseil in Sri Lanka. You walk backwards off the top of a 126-metre cliff with the waterfall thundering next to you. The valley opens up below you as you descend. Professional guides, full safety gear, 3 hours total. Like becoming a superhero — for real.

    ~$60/person (INR 5,100)📞 Ceylon Extreme: +94 70 7900 700
  • 🚗
    11:30 AM — Drive to Kitulgala

    About 80 km, 2 hours on winding mountain roads through tea estates and misty valleys. One of the most beautiful drives in Sri Lanka.

    📏 80 km · ~2 hrs⛽ Petrol Stop 5 in Hatton before leaving · INR 335
  • 🍛
    1:30 PM — Arrive Kitulgala, Lunch

    Vegetarian kottu roti — ask clearly for no fish. Sit by the Kelani River and feel the achievement of the morning sinking in!

  • 🦴
    2:30 PM — Belilena Prehistoric Cave

    People lived in this cave 32,000 YEARS AGO. They found the skeleton of "Balangoda Man" here — the oldest human remains in Sri Lanka. You walk through a rubber estate to reach it. Small, quiet, genuinely mind-bending to stand where ancient humans cooked food and slept.

    ~$2 (INR 170)
  • 🌿
    4:00 PM — Makandawa Rainforest Walk

    Giant ancient trees whose canopy blocks the sky. Endemic birds that exist ONLY in Sri Lanka. The sounds of the forest — cicadas, hornbills, rushing streams. 1 hour walk on marked paths.

    LKR 200 (INR 56)
  • 💦
    5:15 PM — Kataran Oya Natural Rock Pools

    Seven natural pools connected by a 5-metre slide of smooth rock, plus a jumping waterfall. Like a natural water park carved by the river. Perfect way to wash off the abseil adrenaline!

    FREE
  • 🏨
    7:30 PM — Dinner at Riverside Eco-Lodge

    Tell them vegetarian when you book. Many Kitulgala lodges are right on the Kelani River — dinner with the sound of rushing water is perfect after an epic day.

📍 Google Maps
Laxapana Falls Abseil Belilena Cave Makandawa Rainforest Kataran Oya Rock Pools
💡 Tips
🧗After the 126m abseil your legs may feel like jelly — completely normal! Sit, eat something sweet, drink water.
🎬Kitulgala is where 'The Bridge on the River Kwai' (1957 Oscar winner) was filmed!
💡Book the Kitulgala eco-lodge in advance — riverside rooms sell out fast.
Day 7 · 3 April 2026 · Final Day

Rafting & Goodbye

🚣 White Water Rafting · Galle Face Green · ✈️ Home ~84 km
  • 🚣
    8:00 AM SHARP — White Water Rafting, Kelani River

    Grade 2–3 rapids — splashing, screaming, laughing for 5 km down the same river where Bridge on the River Kwai was filmed. 2.5 hours of pure adrenaline and joy. The river is fast, the guides are hilarious, and the jungle on both banks is absolutely beautiful. CONFIRM the 8 AM slot strictly — you have a flight!

    ~$25/person (INR 2,125)📞 Adventure LK: +94 76 686 7707
  • 🚿
    10:30 AM — Shower + Pack + Check Out

    Pack everything carefully — the tuk tuk journey back to Colombo is 84 km on smoother roads.

  • 🚗
    12:00 PM — DEPART for Colombo

    The A7 highway — much smoother than the mountain roads! 84 km, about 2 hours. Last tuk tuk drive of the adventure.

    📏 84 km · ~2 hrs⛽ Petrol Stop 6 · INR 220
  • 🌊
    2:15 PM — Galle Face Green, Colombo

    A long seaside lawn where all of Colombo comes in the evening. Buy a king coconut — your last one! Buy isso vadai (spiced lentil fritters — vegetarian and delicious). Stand at the edge and look at the Indian Ocean one final time.

    FREE30 min stop — don't linger!
  • 🚗
    2:45 PM — Drive to Airport

    35 km from Galle Face Green, about 45 minutes. Drop off the tuk tuk, go to the SriLankan Airlines counter.

    📏 35 km · 45 min
  • ✅
    3:30 PM — Arrive Airport

    You made it — within the 4 PM deadline with 30 minutes to spare! Eat at the CMB departure lounge — plenty of vegetarian options.

  • ✈️
    6:45 PM — Board UL123 to Chennai

    Arrive Chennai at 8:10 PM. 7 days, ~680 km, 2 waterfall abseils, 1 blue whale, countless memories. Trip complete! 🎉

🏨 Hotel Booking Tips
  • 💡 Always tell the hotel one traveller is vegetarian when booking — especially in Maskeliya and Kitulgala
  • 💡 Book the Kitulgala eco-lodge well in advance — riverside rooms sell out fast. Ask for a room facing the river!
  • 💡 Diyaluma guesthouse: Simple and clean. Ask for early breakfast at 8 AM when you check in.
  • 💡 Maskeliya/Hatton: Choose a guesthouse near the town centre for easy petrol access next morning.
