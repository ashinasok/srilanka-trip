⚠️ SCAM ALERT: Always ask the attendant to RESET the pump to ZERO before they start filling. Watch them do it — some stations try to start from a non-zero reading. Also use CEYPETCO (green) or IOC (red) chains — they're the most reliable. A faulty fuel gauge is common in tuk tuks — record your odometer reading at each fill.
💡 Use 92 Octane petrol — it's cheaper (INR 86/L) and works perfectly fine in a tuk tuk. No need to use the more expensive 95 Octane.
Book Before You Fly

🛺 Tuk Tuk Rental Companies

All four companies below handle your Sri Lankan driving permit, give you a driving lesson on Day 1, and offer 24/7 WhatsApp support. Book at least 2–3 weeks before travel — good tuk tuks get reserved fast in peak season (March/April).

⚠️ Indian licence note: As of April 2025, the AA of Sri Lanka only accepts the 1949 International Driving Permit (IDP) — not just your Indian licence. Get your IDP from AA India before you travel. The rental company will then get you a local Sri Lankan recognition permit.
