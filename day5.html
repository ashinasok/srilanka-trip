Highland Wonders

Infinity Pools · Bambarakanda · Lipton's Seat ⭐ · First Abseil! ~110 km

"The most dramatic scenery of the trip — swim on the edge of a 220-metre cliff, stand at Sri Lanka's tallest waterfall, drive through endless tea estates, then abseil 25 metres into a mountain pool."

Day 5 Itinerary

🌊
6:30 AM

Upper Diyaluma Infinity Pools

Natural swimming pools right at the cliff edge — you literally swim and look DOWN at the entire valley 220 metres below. Hike with Guide Saman (+94 71 430 4910) — he knows every hidden pool. 30-min drive up Poonagala Road + 30-min walk.

Guide: ~₹255 Guide Saman: +94 71 430 4910 📍 View on Maps
🍳
8:15 AM

Breakfast + Check Out

Ask for early breakfast at 8 AM when you check in. String hoppers are the perfect highland fuel.

📍 View on Maps
🍳
🏔️
11:00 AM

Bambarakanda Falls — Sri Lanka's Tallest

263 metres — taller than the Eiffel Tower. A thin silver ribbon dropping from the sky into a misty pool. Quick 45-min stop for photos and awe.

Entry: ~₹97 📏 40 km · 1 hr 📍 View on Maps
⭐
12:30 PM

Lipton's Seat — Tea Estate Vista

Drive through endless rolling tea plantations to a hilltop where Sir Thomas Lipton watched over his empire. On a clear April day you can see all the way to the southern coast. The winding road through Dambatenne Tea Factory is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic drives.

FREE 📏 30 km · 45 min ⭐ NEW 📍 View on Maps
🍛
1:30 PM

Maskeliya Lunch

Highland rice and curry — pumpkin, dhal, jackfruit, and cashew curries are spiced completely differently up here. Best vegetarian food of the whole trip!

📍 View on Maps

2:30 PM

GARTMORE FALLS ABSEIL — 25 METRES!

Your FIRST abseil! Walk backwards off a 25-metre waterfall into a pool with the Maskeliya Reservoir glittering below. Sounds terrifying — actually exhilarating. Book with Ceylon Extreme (+94 70 7900 700).

~₹2,550/person Book ahead! Ceylon Extreme: +94 70 7900 700 📍 View on Maps

Maskeliya Reservoir Walk

A glass-calm lake surrounded by misty tea-estate mountains. 45 minutes of peaceful highland walking with cool air around you.
