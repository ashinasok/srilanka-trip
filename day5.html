Upper Diyaluma Infinity Pools

Natural swimming pools right at the cliff edge — you literally swim and look DOWN at the entire valley 220 metres below. Hike up with Guide Saman (+94 71 430 4910) — he knows every hidden pool. 30-min drive up Poonagala Road + 30-min walk. Dawn light is magical.

Bambarakanda Falls — Sri Lanka's Tallest

263 metres — taller than the Eiffel Tower. A thin silver ribbon dropping from the sky into a misty pool. 45-minute stop for photos and awe. In April the falls are at full force. Stunning in every sense of the word.

Lipton's Seat — Tea Estate Vista

Drive through endless rolling tea plantations to the hilltop where Sir Thomas Lipton surveyed his vast empire. On a clear April day you can see all the way to the southern coast. The winding road through Dambatenne Tea Factory is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic drives.
