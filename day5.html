World's End — Horton Plains

A sheer 870-metre cliff that drops straight down into the lowland jungle — nothing in Kerala or the Western Ghats comes close to this. You walk 4.5 km across a cloud forest plateau to reach the edge, then the world just disappears below you. Baker's Falls is on the return path — a 20m cascade through ancient forest. Sambar deer graze the plateau and may walk right up to you. Route from Koslanda: via Haputale → Ohiya → park entrance.

🎟️ Must pre-book online. Foreign visitors must purchase tickets on the Department of Wildlife Conservation portal before arriving — you will be turned away at the gate without them. Book at dwc.gov.lk at least 1–2 days before.

⏰ Cloud warning: Horton Plains clouds over completely most days by 11–12 AM. Arriving at 10:45 AM is borderline — if the plateau is already in cloud, World's End will be a white wall. Baker's Falls is still beautiful in any weather, but the cliff view may be lost. Accept the risk or adjust Day 5 start time if possible.

Castlereagh Reservoir

A vast highland lake ringed entirely by tea estates and blue mountains — the drive around its edge is one of the most scenic in Sri Lanka. At golden hour the mist rolls off the water and the tea-covered hills glow amber. 10-minute detour en route to Hatton, zero cost. Just pull over and breathe it in.
