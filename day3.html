🏝️ ⭐ Must Do 🌊 Walk at low tide

Taprobane Island — Golden Hour Walk

A tiny jungle island 100 m off Weligama Bay, connected to the mainland by a shallow causeway you can walk at low tide. March 30 low tide at 7:46 PM — water is ankle-deep by 5:30 PM. Walk across as the sun turns the ocean gold. Magical.

🌅 ⭐ Must See FREE

Parrot Rock Sunrise, Mirissa

A volcanic island just off Mirissa beach — at sunrise, the first golden light hits the rock face and the ocean turns copper. Almost no one is here at 6 AM. Walk to the rock, sit on the sand, and watch the sky go from indigo to orange. Completely unforgettable.

Coconut Tree Hill, Mirissa

A hill completely covered in tall coconut palms that lean dramatically over the cliff edge — a 5-minute walk from Parrot Rock. Catch the palms lit up in early morning gold light with the ocean beyond. One of those views you'll never forget.
