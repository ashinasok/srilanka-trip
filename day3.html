Weligama — The Big Day

6:00 AM

WHALE WATCHING — Blue Whales at Sea! (3–5 hours)

Blue whales are the biggest animals that have ever lived on Earth — longer than 3 city buses end-to-end. March/April is peak blue whale season off Mirissa. The boat goes far into the deep ocean — bring sunscreen and sea-sickness tablets. Book 2 weeks ahead!

Weligama Town & Shopping

Wander the streets, try fresh tropical fruit, buy a king coconut, pick up a sarong or some local spices. The rhythm of a Sri Lankan fishing town — relaxed and unhurried. About 1 hour.

4:00 PM

Surf Lesson at Weligama Beach — 1.5 hrs

Weligama is called the best beginner surf beach in all of Asia. Gentle waves, brilliant instructors, and you WILL stand up on your first day. Thrilling and hilarious in equal measure.

~₹1,700/person 🕐 Leave by 5:35 PM 📍 View on Maps
🏝️
5:45 PM

Taprobane Island Walk — Golden Hour & Sunset

A tiny jungle island 100 m off Weligama Bay, connected to the mainland by a shallow causeway you can walk at low tide. March 30 low tide is at 7:46 PM — by 5:30 PM the water is just ankle-deep (~18 cm), fully walkable. Stroll across as the sun turns the ocean gold, explore the overgrown jungle paths, and watch the sunset from the far shore. Magical doesn't cover it.

FREE to walk ⭐ Must Do 🕐 Leave by 6:50 PM 📍 View on Maps
