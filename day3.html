WHALE WATCHING — Blue Whales at Sea! (3–5 hours)

Blue whales are the biggest animals that have ever lived on Earth — longer than 3 city buses end-to-end. March/April is peak blue whale season off Mirissa. The boat goes far into the deep ocean — bring sunscreen and sea-sickness tablets. Book 2 weeks ahead!

Surf Lesson at Weligama Beach — 1.5 hrs

Weligama is called the best beginner surf beach in all of Asia. Gentle waves, brilliant instructors, and you WILL stand up on your first day. Thrilling and hilarious in equal measure.

Explore Weligama Town

Wander the streets, try fresh tropical fruit, buy a king coconut, watch stilt fishermen from the shore. The rhythm of a Sri Lankan fishing town.

Parrot Rock Golden Hour

A massive rock island just off the beach that turns deep orange in the sunset light. Sit on the sand and watch the colours shift from gold to crimson. One of Sri Lanka's most iconic sunset views.

FREE 📍 View on Maps
