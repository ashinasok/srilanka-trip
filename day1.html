Day 1 Itinerary

✈️
11:05 AM

Land at Colombo Airport (CMB)

Get a Mobitel or Dialog SIM right inside arrivals. Download PickMe app for tuk tuks. Exchange some currency at the airport — ATMs inside give good rates.

📍 View on Maps
🥥
Noon

King Coconut — Your First Taste!

Bright orange thambili outside arrivals — INR 28 each. Sweet, refreshing, and the perfect welcome to Sri Lanka.

FREE 📍 View on Maps
🥥
⛽
1:00 PM

PETROL STOP 1 — Fill Full Near Airport

CEYPETCO or IOC station on Galle Road before leaving Colombo. 7.5-litre tank — fill it completely. Cost: ~INR 645.

⛽ Petrol Stop 1 · INR 645 📍 View on Maps
⛽
🛺
2:30 PM

Drive South to Bentota — 94 km

Cruise down the beautiful coastal Galle Road with the Indian Ocean glittering on your right. About 2.5 hrs by tuk tuk. Windows wide open — the sea breeze is incredible.

📏 94 km · ~2.5 hrs 📍 View on Maps
🛺
🏖️
5:00 PM

Bentota River Mouth Beach Walk

Where the Bentota River meets the Indian Ocean — a 45-minute walk through golden sand as the sun drops. Your first real Sri Lanka beach moment.

FREE 📍 View on Maps
🚣
6:00 PM

Mangrove River Boat Safari

One hour through tunnels of mangroves. Spot monitor lizards sunbathing on roots, iridescent kingfishers, and giant fruit bats taking off at dusk. Book through your hotel.

~INR 1,700/boat Book via hotel 📍 View on Maps
🍛
7:30 PM

Dinner at Susantha Garden or Ayesha Restaurant

Sri Lankan rice & curry — order "without fish, without meat" clearly. Dhal, pumpkin curry, pol sambol, papadum. Warm, earthy, perfect.
