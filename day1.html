✈️ Airport
✈️ Stop 1

Colombo Airport (CMB)

Get a Mobitel or Dialog SIM right inside arrivals. Cross Immigration. Exchange some currency — ATMs inside give good rates.

Pick up tuk tuk from the airport or Negombo. Make sure you order the tuk tuk licence earlier — Saturday is half day and Sunday is a holiday for the Transport Office. Your adventure begins here.
