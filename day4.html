Dondra Lighthouse — Sri Lanka's Southernmost Point

The southernmost tip of Sri Lanka — a 49-metre white lighthouse built by the British in 1889. If you're leaving Weligama by 8:00 AM, it's a 20-minute detour on your way to Hiriketiya. Stand at the absolute bottom of the island with unbroken ocean in three directions. Worth 20 minutes if you're on time — skip if running late.

⏰ Skip if: you leave after 8:30 AM or the group is slow-moving. Dondra adds 15 km to the day.

Hummanaya Blowhole — Asia's Largest

Asia's largest natural blowhole — seawater blasts up to 23 metres into the air through a narrow underwater cave with every crashing wave. The sound alone is thunderous. It's just 3 km from Hiriketiya (Kudawella village) and unmissable. Go at high tide or rough seas for the full effect — the blowhole doubles in power.

🌊 Tide tip: check Google for "Dikwella tide" before the trip. High tide + southwest swell (March–April) = maximum eruption. Stand well back — waves can soak you without warning.

🏖️ Stop 4 FREE

Tangalle Beach

Wide, wild, and mostly empty — Tangalle's beach is one of the most raw and beautiful on the southern coast. Kick off your shoes and feel the sand before the mountain drive. No vendors, no sunbeds, just open ocean.

💵 ATM stop: Tangalle town has a Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank ATM — withdraw enough cash here. This is the last reliable ATM before the mountains. Aim for LKR 25,000+ to cover the next 3 days.

Buduruwagala Rock Carvings

Seven giant Buddhas carved directly into a jungle rock face 1,200 years ago. The central standing Buddha is 15 metres tall — hidden at the end of a quiet forest path. Genuinely moving to stand before ancient stone this magnificent.
