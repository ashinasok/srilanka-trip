Hiriketiya Beach — Secret Cove

A hidden horseshoe cove only recently discovered by travellers — the ocean wraps around like a perfect turquoise bowl. Shallow, calm, stunningly beautiful. Swim, sit, take photos. One of Sri Lanka's finest hidden beaches. 1–1.5 hrs.

🗿 Stop 3 📅 1,200 years old

Buduruwagala Rock Carvings

Seven giant Buddhas carved directly into a jungle rock face 1,200 years ago. The central standing Buddha is 15 metres tall — hidden at the end of a quiet forest path. Genuinely moving to stand before ancient stone this magnificent.

Ravana Falls — Roadside Wonder

A stunning 25-metre waterfall VISIBLE FROM THE ROAD — just pull the tuk tuk over! Named after the demon king Ravana from the Ramayana epic. Zero effort, completely breathtaking. One of the best roadside stops in all of Sri Lanka.
