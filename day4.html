Into the Mountains

Coconut Hill · Hiriketiya · Ravana Falls ⭐ · Diyaluma ~168 km

Day 4 Itinerary

🌅
6:00 AM

Coconut Tree Hill, Mirissa

A hill completely covered in tall coconut palms that lean dramatically over the cliff edge — watch the sun rise from the Indian Ocean here. One of those views you'll never forget.

FREE 📏 7 km · 15 min 🕐 Leave by 6:35 AM 📍 View on Maps
⛵
6:45 AM

Mirissa Fishing Harbour

Watch the fishermen bring in the morning catch as golden light paints their boats. The most peaceful 30 minutes of the whole trip.

FREE 🕐 Leave by 7:15 AM 📍 View on Maps
🍳
7:30 AM

Breakfast + Check Out

Egg hoppers (crispy bowl-shaped crepes with an egg cooked inside) + pol sambol. Best Sri Lankan breakfast.

🕐 Leave by 9:10 AM 📍 View on Maps
🍳
🏊
10:15 AM

Hiriketiya Beach — Secret Cove

A hidden horseshoe cove only recently discovered — the ocean wraps around like a perfect turquoise bowl. Shallow, calm, stunningly beautiful. 1–1.5 hrs.

FREE 📏 45 km · 1 hr 🕐 Leave by 11:45 AM 📍 View on Maps
🌊
12:00 PM

Tangalle Beach & Lunch Stop

Wide, wild beach. IMPORTANT: say "without fish, without meat, without Maldive fish" very clearly — Tangalle is a seafood town and hidden fish seasoning is everywhere. Petrol Stop 3 here (INR 430).

⛽ Petrol Stop 3 · INR 430 🕐 Leave by 1:30 PM 📍 View on Maps
🍽️
🗿
3:00 PM

Buduruwagala Rock Carvings

Seven giant Buddhas carved directly into a jungle rock face 1,200 years ago. The central standing Buddha is 15 metres tall — hidden at the end of a quiet forest path. Genuinely moving to stand before.

Entry: ~₹425 📏 60 km · 1.5 hrs 🕐 Leave by 4:05 PM 📍 View on Maps
⭐
NEW STOP

Ravana Falls — Roadside Wonder

A stunning 25-metre waterfall VISIBLE FROM THE ROAD — just pull the tuk tuk over! Named after the demon king Ravana from the Ramayana epic. Zero effort, completely breathtaking.

FREE 📏 20 km ⭐ Must Stop 🕐 Leave by 5:15 PM 📍 View on Maps
🏨
4:30 PM

Diyaluma Falls — Check In

Arrive at your guesthouse near Sri Lanka's second-tallest waterfall. Eat early, sleep early — tomorrow morning you'll swim at the very TOP of it.

📏 15 km · 20 min 📍 View on Maps
