The Big Abseil Day

🧗 Laxapana 126m · Prehistoric Cave · Rainforest · Rock Pools ~80 km

"The defining moment of the trip — abseil 126 metres down a thundering waterfall. Then recover in one of Sri Lanka's most cinematic towns among ancient caves, primeval rainforest, and natural rock slides."

Day 6 Itinerary

🧗
8:00 AM

LAXAPANA FALLS ABSEIL — 126 METRES!!

THE moment. The highest free-falling waterfall abseil in Sri Lanka. Walk backwards off a 126-metre cliff with the falls thundering beside you. The valley opens up as you descend — it's like becoming a superhero. Professional guides, full safety kit, 3 hours total. Book with Ceylon Extreme (+94 70 7900 700).

~₹5,100/person MUST book ahead! Ceylon Extreme: +94 70 7900 700 🕐 Leave by 11:15 AM 📍 View on Maps
🚗
11:30 AM

Drive to Kitulgala — 80 km

Winding mountain roads through tea estates and misty valleys. One of the most beautiful 2-hour drives in Sri Lanka. Petrol Stop 5 in Hatton before leaving.

📏 80 km · ~2 hrs ⛽ Petrol Stop 5 📍 View on Maps

Arrive Kitulgala, Lunch

Vegetarian kottu roti by the Kelani River. Let the achievement of the morning sink in while the river rushes past.

🕐 Leave by 2:25 PM 📍 View on Maps

2:30 PM

Belilena Prehistoric Cave

People lived in this cave 32,000 YEARS AGO. "Balangoda Man" — the oldest human remains in Sri Lanka — was discovered here. Walk through a rubber estate to reach it. Genuinely mind-bending.

~₹170 🕐 Leave by 3:20 PM 📍 View on Maps
🌿
4:00 PM

Makandawa Rainforest Walk

Giant ancient trees with canopy that blocks the sky. Endemic birds that exist only in Sri Lanka. The sounds — cicadas, hornbills, rushing streams. 1 hour on marked paths.

LKR 200 (~₹56) 🕐 Leave by 5:05 PM 📍 View on Maps
💦
5:15 PM

Kataran Oya Natural Rock Pools

Seven natural pools connected by smooth rock slides with a jumping waterfall. Like a natural water park carved by the river. Perfect way to wash off the abseil adrenaline.

FREE 🕐 Leave by 6:20 PM 📍 View on Maps
🏨
7:30 PM

Dinner at Riverside Lodge

Tell them vegetarian when booking. Many Kitulgala lodges sit right on the Kelani River — dinner with the sound of rushing water after an epic day.
