🧗 Stop 1 ⭐ THE Moment 📅 MUST book! Ceylon Extreme: +94 70 7900 700

Laxapana Falls Abseil — 126 Metres!!

THE moment. The highest free-falling waterfall abseil in Sri Lanka. Walk backwards off a 126-metre cliff with the falls thundering beside you. The valley opens up as you descend — it's like becoming a superhero. Professional guides, full safety kit, 3 hours total.

⚠️ Fitness note: Maximum weight limit is 100 kg per person. Minimum age 12. Confirm both when booking with Ceylon Extreme (+94 70 7900 700). Also confirm the day before — they occasionally reschedule due to water levels after heavy rain.

Belilena Prehistoric Cave

People lived in this cave 32,000 YEARS AGO. "Balangoda Man" — the oldest human remains in Sri Lanka — was discovered here. Walk through a rubber estate to reach it. Genuinely mind-bending to stand inside.
