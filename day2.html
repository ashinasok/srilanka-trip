7:00 AM

Morning Swim at Bentota Beach

Back to Hotel

Head back to the hotel after your sunrise coastal walk to wash off the sand and salt. Enjoy a final traditional Sri Lankan breakfast—don’t miss the egg hoppers and pol sambol—and fuel up for the long drive ahead. Make sure to double-check your room for chargers and gear before checking out by 8:00 AM to stay on schedule for the mountains

🌿
8:25 AM

Lunuganga Estate (Geoffrey Bawa)

Sri Lanka's greatest architect built this dreamy garden villa on a rubber estate — terraced lawns, ancient trees, pavilions with ocean views. Like walking inside a painting. 1.5 hrs.

Entry: ~₹255 🕐 Leave by 9:40 AM 📍 View on Maps
🎨
10:00 AM

Brief Garden (Bevis Bawa)

Geoffrey's eccentric brother Bevis made this surrealist jungle full of sculptural art and whimsical corners. Even stranger and more magical than Lunuganga. 1.5 hrs to wander.

Entry: ~₹255 🕐 Leave by 11:30 AM 📍 View on Maps
🐢
11:45 AM

Kosgoda Sea Turtle Conservation Project

Baby sea turtles the size of your palm — held gently before being released into the ocean. One of the most heartwarming experiences of the trip.

Entry: ~₹425 🕐 Leave by 12:45 PM 📍 View on Maps
🏄
1:15 PM

Hikkaduwa Beach & Lunch Stop

Beautiful coral reef you can snorkel right off the beach. Great lunch spot — pick up petrol here too (Petrol Stop 2 · ~INR 335).

Beach FREE Snorkel gear ~₹425 ⛽ Petrol Stop 2 · INR 335 🕐 Leave by 2:30 PM 📍 View on Maps
⚓
3:00 PM

Galle Dutch Fort — UNESCO World Heritage

An entire colonial town inside ancient walls. Walk the ocean-facing ramparts, spot the lighthouse, explore the narrow streets lined with boutiques and cafes. Great vegetarian food inside the fort!

FREE entry ~1.5 hrs 🕐 Leave by 4:20 PM 📍 View on Maps
☮️
4:30 PM

Japanese Peace Pagoda, Rumassala

A stunning white stupa perched on Rumassala Hill, built by Japanese Buddhist monks as a symbol of peace. Panoramic views over the Indian Ocean and Unawatuna Bay — a serene, spiritual stop unlike anything else on the coast. ~30 min.

FREE 🕐 Leave by 5:05 PM 📍 View on Maps
🏖️
5:15 PM

Unawatuna Beach

A horseshoe-shaped bay of calm turquoise water — perfect for a quick swim or to just watch the waves. One of Sri Lanka's prettiest beach curves. 45-min stop.

FREE 🕐 Leave by 5:50 PM 📍 View on Maps
🎣
6:05 PM

Koggala Beach — Stilt Fishermen at Sunset

One of Sri Lanka's most iconic sights — men perched on thin poles driven into the sea, fishing as the sun melts into the Indian Ocean. Stop, watch, and photograph this ancient tradition as the sky turns orange and gold.

FREE to watch ~₹170 for a photo op with fisherman 🕐 Leave by 6:35 PM 📍 View on Maps
🛺
7:00 PM

Drive to Weligama — 25 km

Short final leg from Koggala. Check in, rest up — tomorrow you're hunting blue whales!

📏 25 km · ~40 min 📍 View on Maps
