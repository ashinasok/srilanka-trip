Japanese Peace Pagoda — Rumassala

A gleaming white Buddhist stupa perched on a jungle hilltop above the Galle harbour, built by Japanese monks as a symbol of world peace. Short walk through dense forest to reach the pagoda — breathtaking panoramic views of the Indian Ocean and Galle Fort below.

Koggala — Stilt Fishermen at Sunset

Men perched on thin poles driven into the sea, fishing as the sun melts into the Indian Ocean — one of Sri Lanka's most iconic sights. Stop, watch, photograph. The sky turns orange and gold. Ancient, beautiful, unforgettable.
